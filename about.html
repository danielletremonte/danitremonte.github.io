About me

I'm a Senior Product Designer with 10 years of experience building products people actually use. I spent 6+ years at LinkedIn leading design for AI-powered experiences — most recently as the most senior designer on the AI + Intelligence track for LinkedIn Learning, where I worked across MCP integrations, Copilot flows, and AI-native design patterns at a scale of 900M+ users.

Before LinkedIn I designed at American Express, Memori, and Frameology — and before that I spent years in marketing and enterprise sales, which is why I care as much about outcomes as I do about craft.

In 2024 I founded Everydrop — a breastfeeding and lactation app for new moms, live on the App Store, grounded in content validated by certified lactation consultants. I designed it, I launched it, and I now iterate on it using Claude Code. Building a product from zero gave me a different relationship with design decisions: every choice has a cost.

I'm from Brazil and I live in New York. I work in English and Portuguese. I'm looking for my next role designing AI products that change how people work.
